Settings whose switches keep a checkbox in shadow DOM

Expect: opens "Manage preferences", clicks the Analytics and the Marketing switch, once each, and clicks "Save my choices". It clicks neither the checkboxes inside them nor the locked "Strictly necessary" switch.

This is Ionic's toggle: a custom element with role="switch" that listens for clicks, and in its shadow DOM a checkbox that is a switch too. The two are one switch. The element flips its state at once and redraws a moment later, so a click on the checkbox right after the first click would switch it back on.
