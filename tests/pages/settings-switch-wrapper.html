Settings whose switches wrap a checkbox

Expect: opens "Manage preferences", clicks the checkbox inside the Analytics and the Marketing switch, once each, and clicks "Save my choices". It leaves the locked "Strictly necessary" switch alone.

This is Element UI's switch: an element with role="switch" around the real checkbox, which is a switch too. The two are one switch. A click on either reaches the outer one's handler, which flips the value it drew last and draws the new one a moment later, so a second click right after the first would not undo it, but one after the redraw would.
