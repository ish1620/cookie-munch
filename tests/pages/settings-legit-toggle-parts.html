Settings whose switches are an "Off" and an "On" half

Expect: opens "Manage preferences", opens the "Legitimate Interest" tab, clicks the "Off" half of both switches there, and clicks "Save & Exit". It clicks neither the switches themselves nor their "On" halves.

This is the other switch Sourcepoint's panel can have: a button with role="switch" that holds an "Off" and an "On" half, each a switch too, and all three say aria-checked="true". A click on the button itself does nothing; "Off" turns the switch off and "On" turns it on, a moment after the click.
